# Monitor Checklist

- [ ] `MON-01` User feedback and limitations reviewed
- [ ] `MON-02` Data access, consumption, quality, retention, and retrieval monitored
- [ ] `MON-03` Cost, licensing, infrastructure, model, and platform consumption monitored
- [ ] `MON-04` Availability, capacity, reliability, latency, and network performance monitored
- [ ] `MON-05` Accuracy, grounding, fairness, safety, and approved AI measures reviewed
- [ ] `MON-06` Model, prompt, dependency, vendor, API, and platform changes assessed
- [ ] `MON-07` Exceptions and incidents linked to corrective actions and evidence
- [ ] `MON-08` Residual risks, conditions, and expiring exceptions reviewed
- [ ] `MON-09` Continue, improve, pause, or close decision recorded
