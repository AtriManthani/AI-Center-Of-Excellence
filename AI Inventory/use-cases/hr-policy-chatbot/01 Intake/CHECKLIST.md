# Intake Checklist

- [ ] `INT-01` Submitter, department, owner, and sponsor identified
- [ ] `INT-02` Problem and current process documented
- [ ] `INT-03` Affected users, benefits, and baseline measures documented
- [ ] `INT-04` Technology, integrations, data, ownership, and sensitivity identified
- [ ] `INT-05` Initial governance and technical risks captured
- [ ] `INT-06` AI documented as an appropriate option to evaluate
- [ ] `INT-07` Intake confirmed complete enough to qualify
