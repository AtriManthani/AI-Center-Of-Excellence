# Deploy Checklist

- [ ] `DEP-01` Training, communications, and change readiness completed
- [ ] `DEP-02` Production identity, access, network, data, application, and security configuration verified
- [ ] `DEP-03` Monitoring, logging, alerting, escalation, and ownership active
- [ ] `DEP-04` Rollback, recovery, interruption, and incident procedures tested
- [ ] `DEP-05` Stability, capacity, support readiness, and dependencies confirmed
- [ ] `DEP-06` Trustworthy AI and human-oversight controls confirmed
- [ ] `DEP-07` Inventory, risks, approvals, evidence, and limitations current
- [ ] `DEP-08` Deployment decision, date, conditions, and authority recorded
