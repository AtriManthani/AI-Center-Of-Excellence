# Close Checklist

- [ ] `CLO-01` Closure decision, authority, reason, and effective date recorded
- [ ] `CLO-02` Contract, vendor, license, data, and retention commitments identified
- [ ] `CLO-03` Users, integrations, dependencies, and impacts identified
- [ ] `CLO-04` Replacement, transition, rollback, or continuity plan completed when required
- [ ] `CLO-05` Users, data owners, reviewers, and stakeholders notified
- [ ] `CLO-06` Data retention, deletion, records, and audit obligations completed
- [ ] `CLO-07` Accounts, access, secrets, licenses, integrations, and network paths removed
- [ ] `CLO-08` Outcomes, benefits, obligations, and lessons learned documented
- [ ] `CLO-09` Decommissioning independently verified and inventory closed
