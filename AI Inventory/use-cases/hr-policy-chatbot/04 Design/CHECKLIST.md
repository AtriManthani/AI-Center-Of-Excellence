# Design Checklist

- [ ] `DES-01` Cross-functional delivery team and technical owners assigned
- [ ] `DES-02` Current and proposed architecture, environments, and trust boundaries documented
- [ ] `DES-03` Data classification, ownership, lineage, quality, retention, residency, and use documented
- [ ] `DES-04` Data flows, retrieval sources, storage, transformations, and consumers documented
- [ ] `DES-05` Authentication, authorization, service accounts, secrets, and least privilege defined
- [ ] `DES-06` Network segmentation, firewall, ingress, egress, DNS, certificates, proxies, and endpoints reviewed
- [ ] `DES-07` APIs, integrations, dependencies, contracts, errors, and environment separation documented
- [ ] `DES-08` Model, prompt, agent, retrieval, grounding, output validation, and human oversight defined
- [ ] `DES-09` Technical and AI risks assessed
- [ ] `DES-10` Preventive, detective, and corrective controls mapped to material risks
- [ ] `DES-11` Logging, auditability, monitoring, alerting, escalation, and ownership defined
- [ ] `DES-12` Availability, capacity, recovery, fallback, rollback, and continuity defined
- [ ] `DES-13` Success measures, acceptance criteria, thresholds, and review cadence defined
- [ ] `DES-14` Cyber, Data, Application, Network, and Enterprise reviews completed
