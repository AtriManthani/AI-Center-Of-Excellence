# Test Checklist

- [ ] `TST-01` Requirements, criteria, datasets, scripts, and expected outcomes approved
- [ ] `TST-02` Developer, integration, performance, failure, and recovery tests completed
- [ ] `TST-03` Security, identity, access, logging, vulnerability, and abuse tests completed
- [ ] `TST-04` Data protection, authorization, quality, leakage, retention, and lineage tested
- [ ] `TST-05` Prompt injection, hallucination, unsafe output, grounding, and guardrails tested
- [ ] `TST-06` Business-user training and acceptance testing completed
- [ ] `TST-07` Findings triaged, remediated, and retested
- [ ] `TST-08` Production pilot completed when required
- [ ] `TST-09` Pilot results, feedback, limitations, and unresolved risks documented
- [ ] `TST-10` Review groups confirmed conditions are satisfied or carried forward
- [ ] `TST-11` Deploy, remediate, pause, or stop decision recorded
