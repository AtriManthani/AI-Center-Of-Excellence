# Prioritize Checklist

- [ ] `PRI-01` Business value, feasibility, and gravity scored
- [ ] `PRI-02` Technical feasibility and architecture fit confirmed
- [ ] `PRI-03` Platform, infrastructure, integration, and licensing availability confirmed
- [ ] `PRI-04` Data availability, ownership, quality, and access assessed
- [ ] `PRI-05` Delivery capacity, expertise, schedule, and budget assessed
- [ ] `PRI-06` Buy, build, configure, or reuse decision recorded
- [ ] `PRI-07` Priority and sequencing decision recorded
- [ ] `PRI-08` Backlog or deferral rationale recorded when applicable
