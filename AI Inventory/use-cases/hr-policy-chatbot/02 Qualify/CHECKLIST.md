# Qualify Checklist

- [ ] `QUA-01` Technical and organizational feasibility assessed
- [ ] `QUA-02` Costs, benefits, and value assumptions assessed
- [ ] `QUA-03` Initial AI and technical risk assessment completed
- [ ] `QUA-04` Required formal review groups identified
- [ ] `QUA-05` Initial risks mitigated or escalated
- [ ] `QUA-06` Business case confirmed proportionate to risk
- [ ] `QUA-07` Proceed, reject, or return decision recorded
