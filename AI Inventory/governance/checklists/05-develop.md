# Develop

Playbook phase: Solution Development

## Core evidence

- [ ] Business testers identified and required tool training completed
- [ ] Data acquired, processed, documented, and versioned appropriately
- [ ] Training, validation, and test datasets separated appropriately
- [ ] Model, prompts, agents, integrations, and guardrails version controlled
- [ ] Outputs validated in the intended context
- [ ] Trustworthy AI characteristics evaluated
- [ ] Vendor development evidence reviewed when applicable

## Gate questions

- [ ] Is the solution performing as expected in context?
- [ ] Do outputs adhere to trustworthy characteristics?
- [ ] Are the outputs ready for structured testing?
