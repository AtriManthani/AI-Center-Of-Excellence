# Opportunity Identification

## Core evidence

- [ ] Submitter, department, owner, and sponsor identified
- [ ] Problem statement and current process documented
- [ ] Expected benefit and affected users documented
- [ ] Technology, data, and stakeholder context captured
- [ ] Initial risks and governance considerations captured

## Gate questions

- [ ] Is the benefit understood and documented?
- [ ] Is the information required for AI CoE evaluation complete?
- [ ] Is AI an appropriate potential solution?
- [ ] Is the intake valid and complete enough to qualify?
