# Solution Closeout

## Core evidence

- [ ] Closure reason and final decision recorded
- [ ] Contractual and vendor commitments identified
- [ ] Downstream users, integrations, and impacts identified
- [ ] Replacement or transition plan completed where required
- [ ] Affected users, data owners, leaders, and governance stakeholders notified
- [ ] Data retention, deletion, and audit obligations completed
- [ ] Licenses, access, integrations, and production resources removed
- [ ] Final outcomes and lessons learned documented
- [ ] Decommissioning verified
