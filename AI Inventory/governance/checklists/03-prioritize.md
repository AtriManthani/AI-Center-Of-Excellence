# Prioritize

Playbook phase: Opportunity Prioritization

## Core evidence

- [ ] Business value, feasibility, and gravity scored
- [ ] Technical feasibility review completed where required
- [ ] Architecture fit assessed
- [ ] Platform and licensing availability confirmed
- [ ] Development capacity and budget assessed
- [ ] Buy-versus-build decision recorded
- [ ] Backlog rationale recorded when deferred

## Gate questions

- [ ] Has the use case passed applicable technical reviews?
- [ ] Does it fit the existing architecture?
- [ ] Are required licenses available?
- [ ] Is there appropriate expertise and delivery capacity?
- [ ] Is the use case feasible and selected to proceed?
