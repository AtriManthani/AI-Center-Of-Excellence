# Design

Playbook phase: Solution Design

## Core evidence

- [ ] Cross-functional delivery team assigned
- [ ] Current-state and proposed architecture, components, environments, and trust boundaries documented
- [ ] Data sources, classification, access, ownership, lineage, quality, retention, residency, and controls defined
- [ ] Data flows, retrieval sources, storage, transformations, and downstream consumers documented
- [ ] Data Release Committee review completed if needed
- [ ] Authentication, authorization, service accounts, secrets, privileged access, and least privilege defined
- [ ] Network segmentation, firewall rules, ingress, egress, DNS, certificates, proxies, and external endpoints reviewed
- [ ] APIs, integrations, dependencies, interface contracts, error handling, and environment separation documented
- [ ] Model, prompt, agent, retrieval, grounding, output-validation, user-interface, process, and human-oversight requirements defined
- [ ] Technical and AI risks assessed, including data exposure, prompt injection, unauthorized retrieval, hallucination, poisoning, unsafe automation, vendor change, reliability, and model degradation
- [ ] Preventive, detective, and corrective controls mapped to every material risk, with owners and target dates
- [ ] Logging, auditability, observability, alerting, incident escalation, and operational ownership defined
- [ ] Availability, capacity, scalability, recovery, fallback, rollback, and continuity requirements documented
- [ ] Success measures, technical acceptance criteria, monitoring indicators, thresholds, and review cadence defined
- [ ] Cyber, Data, Application, Network, and Enterprise reviews completed or formally marked not applicable

## Gate questions

- [ ] Have Cyber, Data, Application, Network, and Enterprise completed their required reviews?
- [ ] Does every material technical risk have an owner, mitigation, target date, and residual-risk decision?
- [ ] Are high or critical residual risks resolved or supported by an authorized, time-bound exception?
- [ ] Are the data, model, prompt, integration, access, network, logging, resilience, and human-oversight requirements appropriate?
- [ ] Are monitoring indicators and acceptance thresholds defined before development begins?
- [ ] Is implementation approved, approved with recorded conditions, returned for changes, or stopped?
