# Qualify

Playbook phase: Opportunity Qualification

## Core evidence

- [ ] High-level technical and organizational feasibility reviewed
- [ ] Cost and benefit assessment completed
- [ ] Initial AI risk assessment completed
- [ ] Appropriate technical, data, cyber, privacy, and legal reviewers consulted
- [ ] Requester communication documented if the use case will not proceed

## Gate questions

- [ ] Have potential risks been evaluated by the appropriate teams?
- [ ] Can identified risks be mitigated within acceptable limits?
- [ ] Is the business case worth the risk?
- [ ] Is the use case valid or strategically important?
