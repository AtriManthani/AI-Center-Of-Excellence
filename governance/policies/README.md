# Governance Policies

This folder is an index for approved AI-related policies and their controlled locations. It should not contain an unofficial policy presented as approved.

The draft playbook references an AI Acceptable Use Policy, AI Third-Party Risk Management Policy, and AI Risk Management Policy. Add each policy only after confirming its owner, approval state, authoritative location, and review date.

Use `POLICY-INDEX-TEMPLATE.md` to maintain the index.
