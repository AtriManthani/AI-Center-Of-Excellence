# Individual Tool and License Access Process

## Identification

1. A user requests access to an AI tool or feature.
2. Department leadership reviews any departmental cost.
3. The AI COE confirms whether the tool and requested use are approved.
4. Requests outside approved individual use are routed to the use-case process.

## Qualification

1. Confirm license availability and funding.
2. Determine sandbox or production access.
3. Review identity, permissions, and data access.
4. Correct access risks before approval.
5. Assign required acceptable-use, prompt, scenario, or developer training.

## Deployment

1. Confirm training completion.
2. Grant the approved access.
3. Allow use within the approved scope.
4. Route broader departmental opportunities into the use-case process.

## Monitoring

Monitor use, feedback, access, consumption, cost, and identified risks. Remediate through assistance, training, access correction, or tool changes. Remove an unused or unsafe license through a documented decision.
