# Organizational and Departmental Use-Case Process

1. Submit an intake request.
2. Assign a permanent AI ID after validation.
3. Complete qualification and preliminary risk review.
4. Prioritize against value, feasibility, risk, capacity, and budget.
5. Confirm technical feasibility and build/buy path.
6. Form the cross-functional team and complete solution design.
7. Develop or configure the solution with controlled data access.
8. Complete developer, trustworthy-AI, business, and pilot testing.
9. Complete change management, inventory registration, monitoring, and deployment approval.
10. Monitor the deployed solution and address emerging issues.
11. Remediate, replace, resolve, or decommission through an explicit decision.

Every transition must record the decision, decision date, accountable authority, evidence links, conditions, and next action.
