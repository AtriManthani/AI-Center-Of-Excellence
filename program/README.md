# AI Program

## Purpose

This area communicates the health and direction of the AI program at a portfolio level. It is designed for leaders, the AI Governance Council, the AI Center of Excellence, department stakeholders, and other audiences who need a concise view of progress and outcomes.

`program/` summarizes approved information from governance and inventory records. It is not the authoritative location for an individual use case's gate status or inventory metadata.

## What belongs here

- Program strategy and objectives
- Executive and monthly status reports
- Portfolio roadmap and major milestones
- Program-level risks, dependencies, and decisions
- Adoption, training, value, and outcome measures
- Approved communications and success stories
- Program operating cadence

## What does not belong here

- Detailed use-case evidence or gate approvals
- Raw inventory or license records
- Production data or personally identifiable information
- Credentials, secrets, or confidential vendor documents
- Status copied manually from another authoritative record

## Planned organization

As content is added, this area may be organized into:

```text
program/
├── README.md
├── strategy/
├── roadmap/
├── status/
├── kpis/
├── risks/
├── decisions/
└── communications/
```

Every new folder should include a README describing its purpose and maintenance process.

## Reporting principles

- State the reporting period and last-updated date.
- Distinguish facts, forecasts, risks, and decisions.
- Link every use-case-level claim to its governance or inventory record.
- Report trends consistently and explain changes in definitions.
- Use approved measures that connect AI activity to City outcomes.
- Avoid publishing confidential details in an executive summary.

## Update cadence

The final cadence will be set by the AI COE and Governance Council. Until then, cadence references are proposals rather than approved policy.

## Ownership

The AI COE maintains program reporting. Program-level approvals and escalation paths will follow the finalized governance operating model.
