# AI Program Charter

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** AI Center of Excellence

**Last reviewed:** Not yet reviewed

## Purpose

Enable responsible, human-centered use of artificial intelligence to improve City operations, employee effectiveness, service quality, access, resilience, and stewardship of public funds.

## Strategic outcomes

- Modernize and optimize City operations.
- Create conditions for employees to thrive.
- Improve access to City services.
- Support responsible financial stewardship.
- Build organizational AI literacy and capability.

## Guiding constraints for the initial adoption phase

The draft Governance Playbook describes a cautious “Crawl” phase. Its restrictions and guiding principles must be approved before this charter is finalized. Until then, this charter does not authorize any AI use case or override a governance gate.

## Success measures

Measures should balance value and risk, including time saved or reallocated, service quality, user satisfaction, adoption, training completion, cost, reliability, equity, and monitored risk outcomes.

## Governance

Use cases follow the canonical lifecycle documented in `governance/`. Decisions and approvals remain traceable to the permanent AI use-case ID.
